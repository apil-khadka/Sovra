# Compliance boundaries, responsible wording and legal limits

## Claims the prototype may make if actually implemented and tested

- “We run two **simulated** jurisdiction issuers with separate keys/databases.”
- “An eligible Meta model reads mock schema/policy metadata, discovers candidates through typed tools and produces source-referenced plans.”
- “Our verifier rejects cryptographically invalid or revoked demonstration attestations under a documented trust policy.”
- “Users can approve a scoped proof without revealing the underlying synthetic national source profile.”

## Claims the prototype must not make

- It is recognized by Nepal, Australia or any government or bank.
- It is legally KYC/AML-compliant everywhere, or a signed credential equals customer due diligence completion.
- It guarantees all personal information stays in-country; holder-to-RP disclosure, cloud inference and telemetry may be cross-border.
- It uses cryptographic selective disclosure, unlinkable ZK or SD-JWT by merely hiding UI fields or selecting independently signed tokens.
- It implements official OpenID4VP 1.0 or OpenID4VCI 1.0 without actual protocol/profile testing.
- Its model produces genuine chain-of-thought logs or unerringly applies a real jurisdiction's law.

## Law/regulatory analysis to commission before any real deployment

- Authority to issue/accept identity evidence; issuer accreditation, proofing assurance, scope and trust anchors.
- Purpose limitation, storage/egress, holder consent, retention and country-specific processor/transfer requirements.
- Distinguish EU GDPR considerations and eIDAS/EUDI ecosystems from Nepal/Australia local legal duties, not one global rule.
- FATF digital identity guidance and Australian AML/CTF obligations address reliability/risk and regulated entity due diligence; they don't automatically certify a technical integration.
- Government identity document handling and identity fraud threat modeling require specialist legal and privacy review.

This document is architectural risk guidance, **not legal advice**.
